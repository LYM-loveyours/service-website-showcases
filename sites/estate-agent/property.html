Three bedrooms and 1,450 square feet, drawn square-on above with the frontage dimensioned. Switch to the plan for the arrangement, which is what people actually buy on a street like this one.

Three bedrooms and 1,450 square feet. The bay is original, the roof was stripped and re-laid in 2019, and the boiler is four years old with the paperwork in a drawer.

The ground floor works the way these houses were meant to: a front room that holds the bay and the fireplace, a dining room that opens to the garden, and a kitchen wide enough for a table at the end. The utility off the back is where the previous owners hid the washing machine, which is the single most useful thing anybody has done to the house.

Upstairs there are two doubles and a genuine third bedroom rather than a box room — 2.85 by 2.40 metres, which takes a small double and a wardrobe. The loft is boarded with a fixed ladder and has the head height for a conversion, subject to the usual permissions.

Outside: a driveway for two at the front, and about eighteen metres to the rear boundary with a brick outbuilding at the end that is dry and has power.
