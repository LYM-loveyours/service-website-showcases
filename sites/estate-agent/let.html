Morrowell · Letting

Letting it, and looking after the tenancy

Three levels of service, the fees on both sides of the tenancy published in full, and one property manager who knows which boiler is in the loft.

Let only

65%of one month's rentCharged once, on the day the tenant moves in
  • Rent assessment and marketing
  • Photography and a measured floor plan
  • Accompanied viewings, seven days
  • Referencing and Right to Rent checks
  • Tenancy agreement drawn and signed
  • Deposit registered, prescribed information served
  • Rent collection — not included
  • Repairs and inspections — not included

Rent collection

8%of the rent collectedEvery month, for as long as the tenancy runs
  • Everything in Let only
  • Rent collected and paid over within three working days
  • Arrears chased in writing from day three
  • Annual income and expenditure statement in January
  • Rent review at renewal, with the evidence
  • Repairs and contractors — not included
  • Inspections — not included

Fully managed

12%of the rent collectedEvery month, for as long as the tenancy runs
  • Everything in Rent collection
  • One named property manager, and the tenant has her number
  • Repairs arranged up to the limit you set, without ringing you
  • Inspection at three months, then every six, with photographs
  • Compliance dates tracked and renewed before they lapse
  • Deposit return negotiated and evidenced at the end
  • Major works project management — quoted separately

On a rent of £1,400 a month: Let only is £910 once · Rent collection is £112 a month · Fully managed is £168 a month. Every figure includes VAT and is illustrative.

Before it can be advertised at all

Energy performance

A valid EPC before marketing, with the band shown in every advert. We order one during set-up if yours has run out.

Gas safety

An annual Gas Safe record covering every gas appliance and flue, with a copy to the tenant before they move in.

Electrical condition

An EICR at least every five years, with a copy to the tenant within 28 days of the inspection.

Alarms

A smoke alarm on every storey and a carbon monoxide alarm in any room with a fixed combustion appliance, tested on the first day of the tenancy.

Right to Rent

Checked for every adult occupier before the tenancy begins, and repeated where a permission has an end date.

Deposit protection

Registered in an approved scheme within 30 days of receipt, with the prescribed information served.

How to Rent

The current government guide given to the tenant at the start of the tenancy and again at renewal.

Licensing

Selective and HMO licensing vary by ward. We check the council's current scheme for the address before anything is advertised.

General rules for England as at 7 September 2026, written for a design example. Check the current government guidance before relying on any of them.
