Fifteen years, in five lines

Invented history · fictional agency

  • 2011Ellis takes a short lease on a shop unit in Didsbury with one desk, a borrowed camera and eleven years of somebody else's methods to unlearn.
  • 2014The first lettings instruction. The managed book reaches thirty properties by Christmas and the compliance folder becomes a spreadsheet.
  • 2018The Chorlton office opens on Lindenbrook Row, run by two people who both still work here.
  • 2022Measured floor plans and a written photography brief become standard on every instruction, sale or let, whatever the value.
  • 2026Two branches, nine people, and the same rule as 2011: whoever gives you the number does the viewings.

The valuer stays

Whoever gives you the figure does the viewings and rings you with the feedback. No handover to a sales floor you have never met, and no number you cannot trace to a completed sale.

The fee is on the website

Sales, lettings, and both sides of a tenancy, published before you ask. In fifteen illustrative years it has never once cost the agency an instruction.

Bad news travels fastest

If a survey comes back badly or a chain breaks, you hear it the same day, along with what we would do about it and what it is likely to cost.
