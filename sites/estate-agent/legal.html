Privacy notice

Who would be responsible

The data controller would be the registered company named in the company details below. On this example site that name is a slot: no company exists and no registration has been made.

What we collect, and why

From an enquiry form: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your message, the page you sent it from, and the instrument state — the valuation figure or appointment time — but only if you changed it yourself or ticked the box asking us to include it. A sample figure you never touched is not sent.

The lawful basis for answering an enquiry is legitimate interests: you asked us something and we are answering it. Marketing would need your separate consent, asked for plainly, and withdrawable in one click.

How long it is kept

  • Enquiries that go nowhere — 24 months, then deleted
  • Instruction and tenancy records — 6 years from the end of the relationship, for tax and limitation
  • Anti-money-laundering identity records — 5 years from the end of the business relationship

Who else would see it

On a real instruction: a referencing provider, a deposit protection scheme, the solicitors on both sides, the property portals the agency subscribes to, and its accountant. Never a data broker, and never a third party's marketing list.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, exercised by writing to the address in the company details. A response is due within one calendar month.

ICO Information Commissioner's Office — example slot; this fictional agency is not registered and no registration number is claimed. A real agency handling personal data would register, publish its registration number here, and tell you that you may complain to the ICO if it fails you.

Cookies and fonts

This example site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no tag manager. Nothing about your visit is stored on your device by us.

In this draft the two typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google's font service sees the request. The imported build self-hosts both families so no third party is involved at all.

Fees, on both sides

Selling fees, including the minimum fee, the tie-in and what happens if you withdraw, are published in full on the selling page.

Landlord fees and the payments a tenant may be charged are published together on the letting page, because a landlord should be able to see what the tenancy costs the other side before agreeing to it. Under the Tenant Fees Act 2019 an agent in England may only charge a tenant the permitted payments listed there; anything else is prohibited.

Every figure on this site is illustrative and was invented for a design example. VAT is shown at 20% throughout.

Redress and client money

Redress scheme The Property Ombudsman — example fixture; no membership claimed and no membership number exists. A real sales or letting agent in England must belong to a government-approved redress scheme, name it wherever fees are displayed, and tell customers how to escalate to it.

Client money protection Client Money Protect — example fixture; no certificate has been issued. An agent in England that holds client money must belong to an approved client money protection scheme, display its certificate, and say where client money is held and how it is protected.

Deposit protection Approved tenancy deposit scheme — example slot; no scheme joined. A deposit taken on an assured shorthold tenancy must be protected within 30 days and the prescribed information served on the tenant.

These are the fixtures a real agency would place here and beside its fees. Nothing on this page should be read as evidence that any of them applies to anybody.

Complaints procedure

This is the shape of the procedure a real agency would publish. The addresses are examples.

Stage one — the branch

Put the complaint in writing to the branch manager, by email to complaints@morrowell.example or by post to the registered office. It is acknowledged within three working days and answered in full within fifteen working days.

Stage two — the director

If the answer does not settle it, write again and it is reviewed by a director who has not handled the matter. A final viewpoint letter follows within fifteen working days.

Stage three — the redress scheme

If you are still not satisfied, or if eight weeks pass without a final viewpoint letter, the complaint can be referred to the agency's redress scheme. A referral must normally be made within twelve months of the final viewpoint letter.

Written and stated as the general shape of the process on 7 September 2026. A real agency confirms the current time limits with its own scheme before publishing them.

About this example

Morrowell is not a real business. The agency, its people, its properties, its prices, its record and its reviews were written and drawn for a design example by Love Yours Media, a web studio in Manchester.

Telephone numbers come from Ofcom's ranges reserved for drama, so they cannot reach anybody. Email addresses use the reserved .example domain and cannot receive mail. Addresses and street names are invented. The images are authored drawings marked as placeholders, not photographs of anywhere.

The enquiry forms post to Love Yours Media with the subject “example enquiry”. Nothing on this site markets a property, books an appointment or forms any part of an agreement.
