The rosterFolio 30

Ruth Ellery

Partner · accounts and corporation tax · with the practice since it started

Ruth trained in a twelve-partner firm in the city centre and left because she was managing files rather than reading them. She started Tallymere with Idris to do the opposite.

She does the year-end work: statutory accounts, corporation tax, and the meeting before anything is filed. If you are a limited company, she is the one who will sign your accounts off and the one who will ring you in October about the year end.

ruth@tallymere.example

What people ask Ruth

  • What my accounts are actually telling me
  • Whether to take salary or dividends this year
  • Whether the business is worth what I think it is
  • What happens if I bring a second director in

Idris Kwarteng

Partner · VAT, payroll and Making Tax Digital

Idris spent six years doing VAT for contractors and builders, which is where he learned that most VAT problems are really record-keeping problems with a deadline attached.

He owns everything that happens quarterly and monthly: VAT returns, payroll, CIS, and the Making Tax Digital work. He is also the person who will tell you which software to use and, more usefully, which one to stop using.

idris@tallymere.example

What people ask Idris

  • Whether the reverse charge applies to this invoice
  • Whether MTD for Income Tax catches me, and when
  • Why my VAT return does not agree to my bank
  • Whether I should register for VAT before I have to

Nadine Farrow

Client manager · bookkeeping, VAT returns and the weekly work

Nadine keeps the ledgers for about thirty of our clients and prepares most of the VAT returns before Idris reviews them. She is usually the person who answers when you ring.

If something in your records does not make sense, she is the one who notices it in month two rather than month eleven.

nadine@tallymere.example

What people ask Nadine

  • Where a payment went
  • How to get a receipt into the software
  • Whether I have missed anything this quarter
  • Can you just look at this before I send it

Owen Brelsford

Payroll and Self Assessment

Owen runs payroll for every client who has one, and prepares the personal tax returns from August onwards so that January is quiet.

He is the reason we aim to have director returns finished by the end of November. He does not enjoy the last week of January and has arranged his year accordingly.

owen@tallymere.example

What people ask Owen

  • When my staff will be paid and what it costs me
  • What a new starter needs from me
  • What I should set aside for my January payment
  • Whether my pension auto-enrolment is right
