The rules this page computes from, in full. Self Assessment: online return and balancing payment 31 January after the tax year ends on 5 April; paper return 31 October; payments on account 31 January and 31 July. VAT: return and payment one month and seven days after the quarter end. Corporation Tax: payment nine months and one day after the period end; the CT600 twelve months after. Companies House: accounts nine months after the year end for a private company. P11D and P11D(b): 6 July after the tax year ends on 5 April. PAYE and NIC paid electronically: the 22nd of the following tax month — check current HMRC guidance. MTD for Income Tax quarterly updates: 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May — these four were not confirmed against gov.uk for this example; check current HMRC guidance. Working days are not adjusted for bank holidays and a first or shortened accounting period is not modelled.

These are the general rules. Yours may have exceptions.

A first period, a change of year end, a group, or a late registration all move dates. Twenty minutes and last year’s filings is usually enough to map it properly.
