What moves a feeFolio 24

What moves a fee, and what does not.

  • Moves itVolume of transactions, a second trade inside one company, employees, a property portfolio past two, and how far behind the records are when we take them on.
  • Moves itWork we agreed to add during the year. You get the new figure in writing before it starts.
  • Does notPhone calls and emails. A four-minute question is not an invoice line; if it becomes a two-hour piece of work we will say so first.
  • Does notThe month you join. The fee is annual work spread monthly, not a subscription that resets.
